## Scope and activation boundary

R1 adds a stdlib-only, full-screen Harbor Map controller and renderer under
`floati/tui_chart*.py`, a deterministic demo/capture seam, registered DRAFT
copy, and RED-first tests. The controller accepts a caller-supplied declared
snapshot. It performs no discovery and reads no bus root.

`floati chart --live` dispatch is deliberately DARK in this row: `floati/cli.py`
is outside this seat's territory and was not edited. This receipt proves the
runtime seam, rendering, input routing, and degradation behavior; it does not
claim command activation.

No wake, daemon, CLI-dispatch, manifest, deployment, or bus-root file was
edited. The candidate paths are limited to TUI/render/demo, tests, copy ledger,
captures, and this evidence document.

## Study-to-expression trace

`docs/evidence/REGATTA-STUDY-2026-08-28.md` records the grok-build rendering,
focus, mouse, and approval mechanisms and the Cline door mechanisms with
immutable source citations. R1 applies only the working-surface mechanisms:

- one controller owns focus and detail state;
- renderer-produced hit regions route mouse clicks back to semantic targets;
- keyboard movement and Enter are exact twins for mouse selection/detail;
- the runtime writes whole frames through synchronized-output mode;
- rendering is pure and snapshot-fed, while terminal lifecycle stays in the
  controller seam.

The pier/vessel/channel expression, signal lamps, waterline, and copy are
floati's own.

## R1 behavior proved

- Buses render as framed piers, nodes as moored `▤` vessels, architect seats as
  `⚑`, and declared relationships as channels.
- Last-activity brightness survives as `●`, `◐`, and `○` glyphs in every tier.
- Vessel detail names role, inbox, and receipts; pier detail names its ledger
  event count.
- A newly observed envelope produces frames 0, 1, and 2 at 50 ms boundaries,
  expires at 150 ms, and is never replayed from the same snapshot.
- Capture, CI, and non-TTY rendering use settled frames only. The governed R1
  captures intentionally contain no envelope pulse.
- The ordinary loop has no polling interval. A caller multiplexes upstream
  ledger changes into explicit `HarborSnapshotEvent` values; with no pulse
  deadline and no input/state event, the terminal wait is unbounded.
- The selected keyboard target is scrolled into the visible body, and its
  remapped renderer-produced hit region is the mouse target for that frame.
- A stdlib self-pipe turns `SIGWINCH` into a selectable resize event, so a real
  resize wakes the indefinitely blocking loop without restoring a timer. Down
  at the last selectable target enters a bounded tail view where the required
  channel panel and hints remain reachable; Up returns to the selected target.
- 256-color, 16-color, and monochrome renders strip to identical text and fit
  an 80-column viewport; clipped targets do not retain mouse hit regions.
- `NO_COLOR` selects monochrome. A non-TTY caller falls through to the existing
  stable multi-bus chart renderer.
- The normalized v0 seam accepts today's declared-chart topology without
  inventing enrichment: absent node activity/inbox/receipt and pier ledger
  counts render `UNKNOWN`, and absent envelope facts produce no pulse. An
  activated caller must supply those optional measured fields and explicit
  state-change events; this DARK row does not claim that producer is wired.

## Idle measurement

A disposable PTY harness ran the existing Harbor Board and the R1 live map for
10.000 seconds each, drained their terminal output, sent `q`, and waited for a
clean exit. `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1` was set. Results:

| surface | wall | child CPU | exit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| existing Harbor Board | 10.000 s | 0.017285 s | 0 |
| R1 live map | 10.000 s | 0.030864 s | 0 |

Both remain below 0.1 CPU-second over ten wall seconds. More importantly, the
deterministic idle-loop test proves one renderer call and one frame write across
three idle cycles: no ambient render clock and zero idle redraws.
